# Utility Support Slot v0.7.1

## Fixes

- Fixed paired weapons being invisible to the host when using the mod as a
  joining player in a two-player game.
- Fixed incorrect utility animations during those swaps and extra pickup
  messages caused by the new draw sequence.

Tested with a joining player carrying C4 and a Machine Gun, including repeated
swaps in both directions. Joined-player replacement, dropping and recovery,
larger groups, and completely vanilla observers still need more testing.

Your primary weapon may briefly appear during a swap. Other existing limitations
and compatibility advice are listed in the [README](README.md).
